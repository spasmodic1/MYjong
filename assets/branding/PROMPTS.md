# MYjong artwork

Created with the built-in image-generation tool for this project. The PNG masters retain transparency; `tools/prepare-branding.cjs` exports the application icon sizes and WebP assets. No reference photos of people or pets were supplied to image generation.

## Icon prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: final square Windows game app icon for MYjong, a personal mahjong solitaire game. Create one original, beautifully crafted emblem: a softly bevelled ivory mahjong tile inset in a deep jade rounded-square medallion, with a distinctive interwoven M monogram engraved in warm brushed gold and dark jade. The M feels like two interlocking tile shapes, bold and instantly readable at small size. A restrained engraved botanical rim echoes a garden, with tactile ceramic, enamel and fine brass detailing. Contemporary premium casual-game art, polished dimensional illustration, crisp silhouette, balanced centered composition. No words, no tagline, no other letters, no mockup, no multiple variants, no watermark. Outside the rounded-square icon is genuinely transparent. Fill most of the canvas, retain a small transparent safety margin. High visual contrast and simple large central monogram.

## Wordmark prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: final horizontal wordmark for the game MYjong. Reference image is a STYLE REFERENCE ONLY: its ivory ceramic, deep jade enamel and brushed warm gold define the visual family. Generate the wordmark alone, no square icon, no medallion. Exact text, case-sensitive: "MYjong" (M Y j o n g). All six letters must be clearly readable, with a distinctive elegant custom serif design, gentle connected flourishes and subtle dimensional warm-gold edging around luminous ivory letter faces. Keep strong contrast when placed on a dark jade header. A small flowing engraved botanical underline can unite the letters without crowding them. Premium contemporary mahjong solitaire identity; sophisticated and welcoming, not a generic font screenshot. Wide horizontal composition, tightly framed wordmark with a small transparent margin; genuinely transparent background. No tagline, no other words, no watermark, no multiple alternatives.

Reference: `icon-master.png`.

## Garden frame prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: final wide botanical border artwork for the MYjong mahjong solitaire desktop game's playing surface. Make an original premium illustrated frame with hand-painted deep jade leaves, soft ivory blossoms and delicate warm-gold engraved stems, tactile enamel and watercolor-paper character. Composition is wide landscape, approximately 3:2: decorated corners and narrow edge clusters ONLY, particularly lower corners, with the central 80 percent entirely transparent and uncluttered so a mahjong board can sit there. Graceful asymmetry, a few fine curling gold lines, restrained dimensional details matching a luxury ivory-and-jade board-game identity. Sparse enough to remain calm behind large readable game tiles; no straight box border, no characters, no tiles, no text, no logo, no watermark. Genuine alpha transparency throughout the large center and between leaves, no painted opaque backdrop, no solid rectangle. This is a usable production UI asset, not a mockup or screenshot.
