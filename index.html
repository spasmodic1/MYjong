A little guide to MYjong

  1. Find two matching tiles. Click or tap one, then its partner.
  2. Look for open edges. A tile is free when nothing covers it and its left or right side is open.
  3. Take your time. Hint highlights a pair. Undo takes back a move. Shuffle gives the remaining tiles a fresh, solvable arrangement when possible.
  4. Make it personal. Add photos, assign names, and choose identical pictures or different pictures of the same person or pet.

Relaxed play has no score pressure. Challenge and Race the Clock award 100 points per matched pair, with 15 deducted per hint and 100 per shuffle. Challenge records favor points, with time breaking ties. Race records favor the fastest clear, with points breaking ties. Records compare the same starting board size and race interval. Incoming pairs do not remove points already earned.

Keyboard: Tab between controls, Enter or Space to select a tile, H for a hint, Ctrl+Z to undo, and Escape to close a window.

Some choices can leave no matches. Undo or shuffle can help. Challenge scores award 100 points per pair, with 15 deducted per hint and 100 per shuffle.
