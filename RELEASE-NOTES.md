MYjong 0.9.3 adds more ways to make the game yours.

- Choose from five original background tunes: Garden walk, Flowing water, Moonlight, Sunlit morning, and Starlight. Music is optional and uses the volume control.
- Choose Custom color under Background to pick your own playing-surface color. Board text adapts for contrast.
- Add soft jade, powder blue, blush pink, or lavender color to your tiles, alongside the classic finishes.
- A new illustrated jade-and-ivory identity includes an original logo, Windows icon, botanical board artwork, and styled menus.
- Larger Comfortable text is now the default. Settings also offers Standard and Large text, with wider controls and sidebar space.
- Scrollbars are hidden while wheel, touch and keyboard scrolling remain available. A clickable “— More —” indicator appears when additional content is below, and disappears at the bottom. Settings uses a roomier layout.
- Race the Clock adds one matching pair to cleared spaces every 5–120 seconds. Clear the board to win; running out of safe space ends the race. Arrivals pause with the game or an open dialog. Undo covers moves since the latest arrival.
- No legal matches now opens a loss popup. A background analysis can offer rollback to just before the first losing move. If that exact move cannot be proven quickly, only a verified solvable earlier position is offered and labeled accordingly.
- A local high-score board records named results by play style, difficulty, layout, starting size, and race interval. The last saved player name auto-fills next time. Challenge records favor points; Race records favor the fastest clear.
- The phone-upload QR window closes automatically when photos arrive. The collection stays open for review, and remaining selected photos continue uploading. Manually closing a fresh QR window still cancels that session.

Your music and color choices are saved between sessions. Earlier saved games remain compatible.

Use **Settings → Check for Updates**, or install `MYjong-Setup-0.9.3.exe`. Existing photos, names, crops, and saved games are preserved.
