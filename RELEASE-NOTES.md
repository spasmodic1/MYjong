MYjong's first Windows review build brings together offline mahjong solitaire and a personal photo collection.

- Three difficulty levels, multiple stacked layouts, traditional tiles, and photo or mixed collections.
- Identical-photo matching or different photos of the same person or pet.
- Saved games, undo, cycling hints, shuffle, relaxed and challenge play, scores, personal bests, and achievements.
- Local photo import, names and groups, inclusion controls, independent crops for group photos, and backup/restore.
- Local person-face suggestions and pet framing suggestions with bundled offline models. Review all suggested identities and crops.
- Phone uploads over home Wi-Fi, HEIC conversion, background choices, original synthesized audio, and an Update game button.

Install `MYjong-Setup-0.9.0.exe`. The starter collection includes 21 photos of five pets, supplied and approved by their owner for public release. Rename, exclude, or remove them in Photo collection, or add your own photos. Saves and photos stay in Windows app data across updates.

This installer is unsigned. Phone importing requires both devices on the same local network and Windows firewall access on that private network. Recognition can miss faces or suggest incorrect groups; pets are identified manually. New photos you import stay on your computer.
